## Theory

### Distributed genome hypothesis
- Bacterial biofilms in infections
- Genetic potential of the community is not defined by the individual genomes of its members
- Idea of a community gene pool
- Importance of horizontal gene transfer
- Importance of recombination

### Core vs. accessory genome
- Core genome is highly similar to closely related species
- Lineage specific chromosomes
    + Repeat rich
    + High concentration of effector genes
    + Transfer of chromosomes conveys pathogenicity

### What do we by pangenome?
Structural pangenome

Pangenome: gene pool
