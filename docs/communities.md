[YoungCB – RSG](https://www.dtls.nl/youngcb/)

We are an organisation of (PhD-) students, young researchers, and young professionals
in bioinformatics, computational biology, and systems biology in the Netherlands,
who aim to initiate and stimulate scientific discussion as well as collaboration
between young scientists. Our organisation is the official **Regional Student Group
(RSG) Netherlands of the International Society of Computational Biology (ISCB)**.
We strongly believe that there is a lot to learn from each other and there is
nothing more inspiring than working on new ideas together. We closely collaborate
with the Netherlands **Bioinformatics and Systems Biology (BioSB)** Research School
and the ISCB Student Council to achieve this. Embedding in these organisations
allows us to play a stepping-stone role in the national and international communities.

## Graduate schools

Probably most universities have some sort of gradute schools for PhDs.
Some offer resources also for post-graduates and postdocs.

### PE&RC: Graduate School for Production Ecology & Resource Conservation (Wageningen)

The Graduate School for Production Ecology & Resource Conservation is
located in Wageningen and associated with other Wageningen Graduate Schools,
but other organizations are also participating in it.

> NIOO, IBED and Naturalis are participating in PE&RC

PostDoc specific reseurces are available and you can register.
